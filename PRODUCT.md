# SciNexa — Product Truth

**What it is:** An interactive 3D science suite for Egyptian K–12 and Baccalaureate students, mapped to Ministry of Education curricula. Six virtual laboratories: human anatomy, physics/circuits, chemistry/periodic table, biology/cytology, geology/seismology, astronomy.

**Who it is for:** Students (primary → secondary), teachers presenting on classroom smartboards, and school administrators.

**Core jobs:**
1. Explore a lab freely, with real 3D models and simulations.
2. Follow a grade curriculum, complete lessons and weekly assessments.
3. Present a lab on a smartboard without friction.
4. Use the platform offline / from USB.
5. Ask the local, test-mode assistant for pre-written guidance (no external model, no network).

**Product constraints:** Arabic-first RTL with EN/FR/DE; offline-capable; touch + mouse; large 3D payloads; auth via school Microsoft accounts or guest mode.

**Identity direction:** A clinical blueprint on frosted paper. Restrained, academic, instrument-grade; educational colour used only as a subject signal. Clerk-based accounts with student, teacher and administrator roles.
